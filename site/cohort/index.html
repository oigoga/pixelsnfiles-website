Before you join

Questions, answered

I’m a complete beginner — can I actually keep up?+
That’s who this is built for. You don’t need prior experience — just the ability to show up weekly and do the work. Beginners are the norm here, not the exception.
What if I miss a live call?+
Every call is recorded and lives on your learning platform, so you can catch up on your own time. Just try not to make it a habit — your pod is counting on you too.
How much time do I need each week?+
Plan for the weekly live call (around two to two and a half hours) plus a few hours of project work on your own. It’s real work, not a scroll-and-forget course.
I’ve tried freelancing before and it didn’t work. Will this be different?+
Most failed attempts come from having no portfolio, no positioning, and no sales skill — not a lack of ability. This programme fixes exactly those three things.
Can I pay in instalments?+
Yes. Choose the instalment plan at checkout and split the ₦50,000 across payments instead of paying it all at once.
Is the certificate just handed out at the end?+
No. Key pieces of your work are personally checked by your coach before it’s issued — it’s earned, not automatic.

Week seven wraps everything up: certification, a polished portfolio, and a clear next-step plan for landing your first — or next — client.
